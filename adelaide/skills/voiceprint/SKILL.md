# Voiceprint (template)

A voiceprint is what "make it good" looks like for prose, after someone wrote it down. The most common failure is a single undifferentiated document that fits nothing exactly and therefore goes unused — one register asked to cover a client deliverable, an essay, and a Slack message all at once.

This file is an empty structure. Nobody's answers are in it. Fill in each section with your own prompts answered honestly, not with generic advice about good writing.

## Proof

Exemplar passages, one before-and-after pair, a pre-ship checklist.

- Exemplars: `<paste 2-3 passages that are unmistakably yours>`
- Before/after: `<one real edit showing a draft in someone else's voice, and the same passage rewritten in yours>`
- Pre-ship checklist: `<the last few things you check before sending>`

This family matters most. A paragraph that is unmistakably yours gives an agent something to pattern-match against. Adjectives about your voice — "conversational," "direct," "warm" — give it nothing to match against at all.
