What happens, and when.

Times are a guide. The blocks run in this order; how long each one takes depends on the room.

01

Before you come

Two things, both small.

The interview. About a week before the camp you get a link to a short interview about what you are working on and how you work today. It takes fifteen minutes. What comes back becomes the map of the room that Day 1 opens with, so the day starts from what is actually here.

What to bring. Your laptop, and whatever tokens, subscriptions and ways of working you already use — we are not handing out accounts. And a real problem from your own work, the kind you would still be thinking about next week.

02

Day 1 · Articulate

Four shifts, each one ending with something written down.

09:00 – 09:30

Welcome

Opening words, and the map of the room built from your interviews — what everyone here is working on, before anyone introduces themselves.

09:30 – 11:00

Shift 1 · From automation to new work

Automation is where it starts. The value is the work that was not possible before.

Demos of systems that are actually in use, run from the simple to the strange. Then you write your own three hops: what gets faster, what becomes more, and the thing you could not do at all.

You leave with three hops for your own work, one of them circled.

11:00 – 11:15

Break

11:15 – 12:30

Shift 2a · Articulation is the bottleneck

You cannot delegate what you cannot articulate.

Exact instructions, with real bread. You write down how to make a sandwich; a partner follows what you wrote, exactly as written. Everyone eats the results.

You leave with your own method, in two sentences.

12:30 – 13:30

Lunch

13:30 – 14:45

Shift 2b · Make it explicit

Written down, a method becomes challengeable, improvable, re-runnable.

What explicit looks like on disk: a voiceprint, a design system, a pipeline someone else on your team can run. Then you take a multi-step process from your own work and break it into its parts.

You leave with your pipeline, decomposed — the head start for tomorrow.

14:45 – 15:00

Break

15:00 – 16:00

Shift 3 · From individual to collective capability

Explicit expertise is coordinable.

Everything so far was built for one person. This block widens it to teams: shared protocols, agents as members of a working space, and what the investment buys once more than one person can run it.

You leave with an idea worth a day of work.

16:00 – 16:30

Find your tribe

You pick who you are building with tomorrow, or decide to work solo. Both are first-class, and both start from what you wrote down today.

16:30 – 16:45

Close

16:45 →

Drinks, same place

03

Day 2 · Build and demo

The day belongs to the teams. The shape is set; the cuts inside it are called on the day.

09:00

Build

Teams arrive already paired and start. Heads-down work on the idea you seeded yesterday, with the two of us roaming — theory comes out on demand, when you hit something that needs naming.

mid-day

Check-in

Each group says where it is in a sentence or two, then everyone goes back to work.

afternoon

Share-out

Three or so groups show what they built. Everyone says out loud what they learned — that is the documentation of the two days.

close

We finish in time to travel
