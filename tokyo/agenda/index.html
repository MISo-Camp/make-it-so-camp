What and when.

01

Before you come

An interview, and what to bring.

The interview. About a week before the camp you get a link to a short interview, led by an AI agent. It takes about fifteen minutes and runs as a conversation: it asks what you are working on and how you work today, and you answer in your own words. The answers become the map of the room that Day 1 opens with, so the two days start from what everyone here actually does. Your answers are used only to prepare the workshop.

What to bring. Your laptop, and the AI tools you already work with, signed in and ready. Bring a real problem from your own work, the kind you would still be thinking about next week.

02

Day 1 · Articulate

Four shifts.

09:00 – 09:30

Welcome

Opening words, then the map of the room built from your interviews: what everyone here is working on.

09:30 – 11:00

Shift 1 · From automation to new work

Automation is where it starts. The value is the work that was not possible before.

Demos of systems that are actually in use, run from the simple to the strange. Then you write your own three hops: what gets faster, what becomes more, and the thing you could not do at all.

Takeaway: a clearer picture of where AI actually changes your work, and one opening worth pursuing.

11:00 – 11:15

Break

11:15 – 12:30

Shift 2a · Articulation is the bottleneck

You cannot delegate what you cannot articulate.

Exact instructions, with real bread. You write down how to make a sandwich; a partner follows what you wrote, exactly as written. Everyone eats the results.

Takeaway: a first-hand sense of how much of your own method you have never put into words.

12:30 – 13:30

Lunch

13:30 – 14:45

Shift 2b · Make it explicit

Written down, a method becomes challengeable, improvable, re-runnable.

What explicit looks like on disk: a voiceprint, a design system, a pipeline someone else on your team can run. Then you take a multi-step process from your own work and break it into its parts.

Takeaway: your own way of working written down, in a form you or your team can run again.

14:45 – 15:00

Break

15:00 – 16:00

Shift 3 · From individual to collective capability

Explicit expertise is coordinable.

Everything so far was built for one person. This block widens it to teams: shared protocols, agents as members of a working space, and what the investment buys once more than one person can run it.

Takeaway: a sense of what your method is worth once a team can run it, and an idea to build tomorrow.

16:00 – 16:30

Find your tribe

You pick who you are building with tomorrow, or decide to work solo. Both are first-class, and both start from what you wrote down today.

16:30 – 16:45

Close

16:45 →

Drinks, same place

03

Day 2 · Build and demo

A day of building. We are there to spar and guide.

09:00

Build

Teams arrive already paired and start. Heads-down work on the idea you seeded yesterday. We move between the groups; ask us when you hit something that needs naming.

mid-day

Check-in

Each group says where it is in a sentence or two, then everyone goes back to work.

afternoon

Share-out

Three or so groups show what they built, and everyone says out loud what they learned.

close

We finish in time to travel
